At She2She Media, we believe in creating an inclusive and welcoming digital experience for all Boss Ladies, business owners, and visitors—regardless of ability. We are committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

## Our Commitment

We strive to meet or exceed the standards set forth in the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We are continuously working to improve the usability and accessibility of our site to make it easy for all users to navigate and interact with our content.

## Accessibility Features

To support accessibility, our website includes:

- Keyboard navigation compatibility
- Text alternatives for images (alt text)
- Color contrast for readability
- Responsive design for screen readers and mobile devices
- Clear and consistent navigation structure

## Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we’re always learning. We regularly review our website for accessibility issues and aim to address them promptly. As our services and content evolve, we will continue to enhance accessibility features.

## Need Help Accessing Something?

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website or have suggestions on how we can improve accessibility, please don’t hesitate to contact us. We’re here to help!

Email: [help@she2shemedia.com](mailto:help@she2shemedia.com)
Website: www.she2shemedia.com

We strive to respond to all accessibility-related requests within 2–3 business days.
