**The She2She "Actually Free" Website Giveaway**
No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not increase your chances of winning.

## 1. Sponsor

The giveaway is run by She2She Media LLC ("She2She Media", "I", "me"). Questions can go to [anita@she2shemedia.com](mailto:anita@she2shemedia.com).

## 2. Who can enter

The giveaway is open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older when they enter, and who own or co-own a women-owned business. "Women-owned" means at least 51% owned by one or more women. Businesses that already have a website and businesses that don't are both eligible.

Not eligible: She2She Media's current clients with an active website project, and members of my immediate family or household. Void where prohibited by law.

## 3. Entry period

Entries open when this giveaway is published on November 25, 2026, and close on **Monday, November 30, 2026 at 11:00am Eastern Time**. Entries received after that are not counted.

## 4. How to enter

Fill in the entry form at [she2shemedia.com/giveaway](/giveaway) with your name, email, business name and current website (if you have one). Entering is free.

If the form doesn't work for you, you can enter by emailing the same details to [anita@she2shemedia.com](mailto:anita@she2shemedia.com) with "Giveaway entry" in the subject line, before entries close. Both methods count the same.

Limit one entry per person and one per business. Extra entries are removed before the draw. Entries made with fake details or automated tools are disqualified.

Entering adds you to the She2She Media email list. You can unsubscribe at any time, and unsubscribing doesn't affect your entry.

## 5. Choosing the winner

On **Monday, November 30, 2026 at 12:00pm Eastern Time**, I'll number every eligible entry in the order it was received and choose one winner with a random number generator. The draw will be recorded. Your odds of winning depend on how many eligible entries there are.

## 6. Notifying the winner

I'll email the winner at the address they entered with by December 1, 2026. The winner has **10 days** from that email to reply and confirm they're eligible. If the winner doesn't reply in time, isn't eligible, or declines the prize, I'll draw another winner from the remaining entries.

## 7. The prize

One winner receives one 3-page website designed and built in code by She2She Media. Approximate retail value: **$1,300**. The prize includes:

- A new 3-page website (for example Home, About and Contact) in the winner's existing brand
- Mobile-first design, a contact form or booking link, and basic SEO
- Website copy written by She2She Media, or the winner's own copy if they already have it
- 14 days of post-launch fixes, then a handover to the winner's own accounts

**Ownership.** When the website is finished and handed over, the website and all of its files belong to the winner, with no strings attached. There is no contract, subscription or ongoing fee. After handover, She2She Media has no obligation to make further changes. Future updates can be made through She2She Media's website care plan at its regular price, or the winner may give their files to any developer of their choice.

The prize does **not** include: a domain name or domain renewal; branding, a logo, or brand design (the winner's colors, fonts and logo need to be decided already); photography; additional pages; a Wix build; changes to or migration of an existing website; or ongoing website care. Optional services, such as website care or branding, are available at She2She Media's regular prices and are never required.

**Timeline.** I aim to have the website live by December 31, 2026. That depends on the winner sending their logo, brand details, any existing copy and photos, and their feedback promptly. If the winner doesn't respond to project emails for 30 days, the prize may be forfeited.

The prize can't be exchanged for cash or transferred to another person or business. I may substitute features of equal or greater value if something listed isn't available.

## 8. Taxes

The winner is responsible for any taxes on the prize. Please talk to a tax professional if you have questions about how this affects you.

## 9. Testimonial and showing the finished website

The prize costs the winner no money. In return, as a condition of accepting the prize, the winner agrees to:

- Provide a short written testimonial about working with She2She Media once the website is live
- Allow She2She Media to show the finished website, the testimonial, the business name and the winner's first name on the She2She Media website and social media, without further payment
