**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

She2She Media, LLC is a one-person business run by me, Anita. This policy explains, in plain language, what information this website (www.she2shemedia.com) collects, what I do with it, and which outside services are involved. If anything here is unclear, email me at [anita@she2shemedia.com](mailto:anita@she2shemedia.com).

## 1. The short version

- I only collect what you choose to send me, plus anonymous visitor counts.
- I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't use advertising trackers.
- This website doesn't set any cookies of its own. The only cookies come from the Calendly booking calendar, and only on the booking page.
- You can ask me to show you, correct or delete what I hold about you at any time.

## 2. What I collect and why

**When you contact me or request a free audit.** The "Send a note" form and the free website audit form ask for your name, email address, and your business name or website, plus anything you write in the message box. I use this to reply to you and, for audits, to review your site and email you the results.

**When you book a consultation.** Booking through the calendar collects your name, email address and any answers you give to the booking questions. I use this to schedule and hold the call, which takes place on Zoom.

**When you join my email list.** If you tick the box to receive website and marketing tips, or sign up another way, I'll add your name and email address to my mailing list. I'll only email you if you've opted in, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

**When you message me through the chat button.** The chat button on this site doesn't collect anything itself. It opens WhatsApp or your email app, so the conversation happens there, under that service's own privacy terms.

**When you become a client.** I'll hold the details we need to work together, such as contact information, project materials, account access you give me, and invoicing records.

**Anonymous visitor statistics.** I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see things like how many people visit, which pages they read, which country they're in and what kind of device they use. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a profile of you, and doesn't track you across other websites.

## 3. The services that handle your information

I use a small number of trusted services to run the business. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job:

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website and provides the visitor statistics. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure.
- **Web3Forms** delivers messages from the contact and audit forms to my inbox.
- **Zoho** handles my email (Zoho Mail), my mailing list (Zoho Campaigns) and client invoices. If you pay an invoice by card, the payment is processed by Zoho's payment partner; I never see or store your full card details.
- **Calendly** runs the booking calendar, and **Zoom** hosts the consultation calls.
- **WhatsApp** (Meta) handles any conversation you start there.

I don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn't set cookies of its own, and there's no advertising or cross-site tracking.

**Calendly** sets cookies when its booking calendar loads on the [booking page](/book-online). Visitors from regions that require consent, including the UK and EU, are asked before those cookies are set.

The site also remembers two small things in your own browser, so it doesn't keep repeating itself: whether you've closed the free audit popup, and whether the chat button has already said hello. This stays on your device, isn't sent to me, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

## 5. How long I keep it

- **Messages and audit requests:** as long as needed to reply, and for a reasonable time afterward in case you follow up.
- **Mailing list:** until you unsubscribe or ask me to remove you.
- **Client records:** for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet tax and accounting requirements.

## 6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me to:

- Tell you what information I hold about you, and give you a copy
- Correct anything that's wrong
- Delete your information
- Stop sending you marketing emails (the unsubscribe link works too)

If you're in the UK or EU, you also have the right to object to or restrict how your information is used, and to complain to your local data protection authority. I rely on your consent for the mailing list, and on our need to communicate for everything else you've asked me to do, like replying to a message or running an audit.

To make any of these requests, email [anita@she2shemedia.com](mailto:anita@she2shemedia.com). I'll respond within 30 days.

## 7. Keeping it safe

Your information is stored with the reputable services listed above, and I keep access to my accounts protected. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, but I take reasonable care to protect what you share.

## 8. Children

This website and my services are for business owners. They aren't aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

## 9. Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as client sites on the [Work](/work) page and social media profiles. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy doesn't cover them.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your information, I'll update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
