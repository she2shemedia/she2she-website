Welcome to She2She Media (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, www.she2shemedia.com (the “Site”), or interact with our services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Information:** When you fill out a contact form, sign up for our email list, schedule a consultation, or work with us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, and other information you provide.
- **Automatically Collected Information:** We may collect data about your device and use of the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent, using cookies or third-party analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Respond to inquiries and provide services
- Send you updates, newsletters, and marketing communications (only if you opt-in)
- Improve our website, services, and user experience
- Analyze website performance and user engagement

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who help operate our business (e.g., email marketing tools, analytics providers), but only as necessary and with appropriate safeguards.

## 4. Cookies

Our Site uses cookies to improve functionality and user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the Site may not function properly.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to the personal data we hold about you
- Ask us to correct or delete your personal information
- Opt out of receiving marketing emails at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: [rights@she2shemedia.com](mailto:rights@she2shemedia.com)

## 6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your personal information as well.

## 8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we’ll update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site means you accept those changes.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact: [info@she2shemedia.com](mailto:info@she2shemedia.com)
